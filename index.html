Who We Are

Built for Berbera.
Built for the Future.

Hanaqaad College was founded with a singular vision: to provide the youth of Berbera with the technical and professional skills needed to compete in today's global economy.

Located near Al-Nuur in Berbera, Somaliland, Hanaqaad College bridges the gap between ambition and opportunity. Our curriculum is meticulously crafted by industry professionals and educators who understand both the local context and global standards.

From hands-on computer labs running cutting-edge software, to language labs designed to cultivate English and Arabic fluency, every resource at Hanaqaad is purpose-built to produce graduates who are confident, capable, and career-ready.

We offer flexible scheduling, affordable tuition, and certificates recognized by employers across Somaliland and the Horn of Africa.
